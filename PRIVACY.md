# Privacy Policy — twisted4d

Last updated: 2026-08-01

twisted4d does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, from you or about you.

- **No permissions requested.** The app's Android manifest requests zero runtime or
  install-time permissions.
- **No network access.** twisted4d makes no network requests of any kind — no analytics, no
  crash reporting, no advertising, no account system, no cloud sync.
- **No data collection.** Puzzle state (scramble progress, settings, move history) is saved
  locally on your device only, in the app's private storage, and is never transmitted anywhere.
  Uninstalling the app removes this data completely.
- **No third-party SDKs.** twisted4d's only dependencies are standard AndroidX UI libraries; no
  advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries are included.
